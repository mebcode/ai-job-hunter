# Job hunter setup (intake interview)

1. Read `guardrails/GUARDRAILS.md` and follow it throughout. Copy `templates/candidate-profile.template.md`; the filled copy is this skill's only output.
2. Ask the user to share their current resume. Read it and fill every field it answers: work history with exact dates, education, certifications, skills.
3. Ask about the rest one topic at a time, in plain questions, never as a long form:
   - Target roles: core titles, close cousins, and what counts as a wildcard.
   - Work they prefer and work they want to avoid.
   - Location: home city, commute limit (miles or minutes), on-site, hybrid, or remote, and whether they'd relocate.
   - Salary floor, annual. If they think hourly, store both (hourly × 2,080).
   - Start date window and employment types.
   - Schedule limits: school nights, caregiving, a fixed end of day.
   - Level, and which years-of-experience requirements are too high.
   - Skill levels, stated honestly for each: expert, working, or familiarity/coursework.
   - Deal-breakers and soft factors (development programs, tuition help, commute, schedule fit).
   - Optional, only if relevant: clearance level and status, polygraph history and willingness, and whether to search government boards.
   - Standard application answers (work authorization, veteran or disability status, EEO answers, relocation). Tell them "decline" or "ask me each time" is fine for any of these.
   - References: names and relationship only. Never store their phone numbers.
4. Fill the template. Delete sections that don't apply. Never store an SSN, student ID, birthdate, or street address; the city is enough.
5. Read back target roles, location, salary floor, start date, and deal-breakers in five lines or fewer, and ask "Is this right?" Fix anything wrong.
6. Save it at `<PROFILE_LOCATION>` (agent memory, a file, or a Notion page; see the profile's "Where things live" section). Tell the user where it lives.
7. Later, when the user says something changed, update only that field, read the change back, and save.
