## Ideas for building the list
- Employers in your field with offices inside your commute radius.
- Companies where people with your background work (check alumni pages or "people also viewed").
- Local hospitals, universities, school systems, and government agencies, which are big employers in most areas.
- Optional, for cleared or defense work: prime contractors, federally funded research centers, and labs with sites near you.
